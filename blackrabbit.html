Welcome

A timeless bar
& dining destination

Tucked away in the heart of Chester-le-Street, Black Rabbit is inspired by the elegance of classic English hospitality, with subtle influences from the great French bistros and brasseries.

Designed to feel both luxurious and relaxed, Black Rabbit blends rich interiors, warm lighting and intimate surroundings — a space equally suited to long lunches, late-night cocktails, special occasions and unforgettable evenings around the table.

Refined without pretence, every detail has been carefully considered to deliver an atmosphere that feels sophisticated, welcoming and effortlessly timeless.

Elegant. Relaxed. Timeless.
