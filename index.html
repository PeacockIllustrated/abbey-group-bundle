01 — The opportunity

A family of houses,
told as one

The Abbey Group has built something rare — a collection of genuinely distinctive venues across the North East.

But online, that stature isn't showing. Each site sits on its own, in its own style, on its own dated template — and none of them speak to each other. A guest who loves Black Rabbit has no reason to discover Hibou Blanc. The group's reputation outpaces its digital presence.

That gap is the opportunity: present the houses the way they deserve, and connect them so the whole is greater than its parts.

02 — The vision

One banner.
Every house.

A central Abbey Group hub sits above the family — a single front door for reservations, events, private dining and gift cards.

Beneath it, every venue gets its own world-class site: distinct in character, but sharing a design language, a level of craft and a booking flow. Guests move between the houses effortlessly. The group finally looks like the group it is — and every marketing pound works six times as hard.
