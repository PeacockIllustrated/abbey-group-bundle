The Building

Sunlight Chambers

A Grade II listed building on the corner of the Bigg Market and High Bridge Street, built in 1902 for the Lever Brothers by nationally renowned architect W. S. Owen.

Named after the Lever Brothers' most famous product - Sunlight Soap - this quirky, curiously coloured building carries a remarkable mosaic frieze depicting work and honest toil.

Inside is every bit as beautiful as out. We've woven the building's original features into the design of Hibou Blanc, creating a room that oozes old-school style and glamour.

The Kitchen

Our menu is lovingly crafted by Head Chef Neil Jefferson, reflecting the very best of local, seasonal produce - changed often to keep it so.
